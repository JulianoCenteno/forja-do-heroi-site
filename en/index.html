Frequently asked questions

Get your answers and everything you need to know.

What is Hero's Forge?

Hero's Forge is a gamified workout app inspired by RPGs and leveling anime. It turns your physical progress into a hero's journey with workouts, quests, bosses, rewards and level-ups.

Do I need a gym to use the app?

No. The Monk class trains with bodyweight only, at home or at the park. If you train at a gym, the Warrior logs weight and earns more XP for what you lift. The Paladin mixes both.

Does the app come with ready-made workouts?

With Elite Hunter, yes: the 30-day Dungeon gives you each day's workout ready for your class, starting light and ramping up every week. With Recruit, you pick the exercises (45+, with levels I to V) and the app turns every set into XP, quests and bosses.

What's the difference between Recruit and Elite Hunter?

Recruit has the essentials: you log your workouts and the app turns everything into XP, quests, bosses and progress. Elite Hunter includes all of that plus the Masters, GPS running, the Tavern, Hero's Alchemy (high-protein recipes), workout sheets and advanced protocols.

Can I cancel anytime?

There's no subscription to cancel: you pay once and the access is yours. And if you don't like it, request a refund within the first 7 days and get 100% back, no hassle.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payment is processed in a secure environment by card. And you get a 7-day unconditional guarantee: if you don't like it, request a refund and get 100% back.
